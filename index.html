Our Heritage & Calling

“Biyaya means blessing, and every good gift comes from God.”

Discovering that pure cacao provides a worry-free, healthier choice for all to enjoy chocolate motivated us to believe in authentic cacao tableas.

Philippine-grown cacao holds deep historical wonder—a crop that originated in South America yet thrives beautifully right here in our country, the first Asian country where cacao was planted.

Witnessing the heart, patience, and dedication our local farmers and artisans pour into every single batch moved us deeply. By building Biyaya, we are honored to work hand-in-hand to support their livelihoods while keeping this beloved heritage alive.

Brewing Tradition

Cooking Instructions

Bring out the aromatic oils and velvety texture with these simple, authentic pot-simmer steps.

Pure Blend 1 Serving = 1 Tablea
1
Add Water

Pour 300 mL of water into a pot.

2
Add Tablea

Add 1 tablea directly into the water.

3
Simmer Uncovered

Simmer over medium to low heat for 10–12 minutes or until fully melted.

4
Finish & Stir

Add sweetener or milk as desired. Stir well before serving.

Bittersweet Blend 1 Serving = 2 Tableas
1
Add Water

Pour 250 mL (1 mug) of water into a pot.

2
Add Tableas

Add 2 tableas into the pot.

3
Simmer Uncovered

Simmer uncovered on medium-low for 10–12 minutes until dissolved.

4
Customize

Add milk or sweetener as desired. Stir vigorously with a whisk or batirol.

Sweet Blend 1 Serving = 2 Tableas
1
Add Water

Pour 250 mL (1 mug) of water into a pot.

2
Add Tableas

Add 2 tableas to the water.

3
Simmer Uncovered

Simmer uncovered over medium-low for 10–12 minutes until melted.

4
Add Milk & Enjoy

Add fresh or condensed milk as desired. Stir well before serving.
